How to Answer a Birthday Wish Without Sounding Flat

Most people answer a birthday wish with one word: "Thanks." It is polite, and it ends the conversation. A better answer takes one more sentence, and it names something real.

If they wrote a long message, answer the part that took effort. "Thank you — the part about our old flat made me laugh" says more than "Thanks so much!" ever will. If they only wrote "Happy birthday", a short warm line back is enough. Match their effort, then add one true detail of your own.
