A Metaphor Is a Shortcut, Not a Decoration

A good metaphor saves time. It takes a hard idea and puts it next to something the reader already knows, so the idea lands in one sentence instead of a paragraph. That is the whole job.

It stops working when you add it only to sound clever. If the comparison does not make the idea clearer, it is just extra weight. One clear comparison, then plain words for the rest. Say the idea, show it once, and move on.
