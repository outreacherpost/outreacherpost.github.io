One Line to Read Before a Hard Day Starts

A hard morning does not need a long speech. It needs one line you can hold onto until the day gets easier. Pick it the night before, not in the rush of the morning.

The line should be short enough to remember without looking, and true enough that you believe it on a bad day, not only on a good one. Read it once, put the phone down, and start with the smallest job in front of you. A line cannot fix the day, but it can give you a place to start it.
