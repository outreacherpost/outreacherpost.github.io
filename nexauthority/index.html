Happy, Glad, Delighted — Why the Small Difference Matters

"Happy", "glad" and "delighted" all talk about joy, but they are not the same word. "Glad" is quiet and polite. "Happy" is warm and general. "Delighted" is stronger — surprised joy, the kind that shows on your face.

Use "glad" in a short message to a colleague. Use "happy" when you mean it plainly. Save "delighted" for the news that really surprised you. If you use the strongest word for everything, it stops meaning anything.
